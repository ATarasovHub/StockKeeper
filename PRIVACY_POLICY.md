# StockKeeper Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

StockKeeper is an offline inventory application. It stores product records, inventory transactions, customer details, notes, and product photos locally on the user's device.

## Data collection and sharing

StockKeeper does not send personal data, inventory data, analytics, advertising identifiers, crash reports, or usage information to the developer or to third-party services. The app does not contain advertising or tracking SDKs.

## Device permissions

StockKeeper requests camera access only when the user chooses to take a product photo. Existing photos may be selected through Android's system photo picker.

## Storage, backup, and export

Application data is stored in the app's private storage. Automatic Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled. Users can explicitly export:

- a ZIP backup containing the database and product photos; or
- an Excel workbook containing database tables.

Exported files are not encrypted by StockKeeper. After export, the user controls where these files are stored or shared and is responsible for protecting them.

## Data deletion

Users can remove individual directory records where allowed by inventory history. Uninstalling StockKeeper removes data held in the app's private storage. Files previously exported by the user must be deleted separately from their chosen storage location.

## Children's privacy

StockKeeper is a general-purpose business inventory tool and is not directed to children.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the application's data practices change. The latest version will be made available through the Google Play listing and inside the application.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the support email shown on the StockKeeper Google Play listing.
